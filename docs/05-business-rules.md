## Overview

Rules governing the qualification modal (email first, then optional questions on the same lead), lead handling, and language constraints. **The lead path's durable record is the Google Sheet, not an email** (Sprint 02; `07-technical-spec.md`).

## Glossary

- **Lead** — step 1 of the modal (`kind: "quick"`, `source: "quick_door"`): first name, last name, email and at least one of the two offerings, durably recorded under a server-minted **lead ID**. Everything else the visitor says attaches to it. **CHANGED (decision-log #65).**
- **Follow-up** — step 2 of the modal (`kind: "followup"`): the four qualifier answers, company and "tell us about it", every one optional, sent with the step 1 lead ID and email so it **updates that lead's Sheet row** instead of adding one. **CHANGED (new, #65).**
- **Quick lead** — a lead that stopped at step 1 (`lead_type: quick`, `[Lead — quick]`). **CHANGED (#65):** before, the quick door's own completion (name + email + optional needs).
- **Qualified lead** — a lead whose step 2 answered all four questions with no Rule 2.5 flag (`lead_type: qualified`, `[Lead]`). **CHANGED (#65):** the `kind: "qualified"` payload retired; the qualifier's answers ride the follow-up.
- **Off-ramp** — step 2's ending when an answer is "I'm still exploring" or "Just exploring" (Rule 2.1); the answer is recorded on the lead and the honest "not yet" message is shown. **CHANGED (#65):** its own email capture (`source: "off_ramp"`) retired, since step 1 already has the email.
- **Waitlist entry** — an email left in the home page's waitlist band for the installable product (`kind: "waitlist"`, decision-log #58). **Not a lead**: it rides the lead seam for its durable record, guards and fallback, and carries its own `[Waitlist]` label. **CHANGED (new).**
- **Escape hatch** — the always-visible `hello@nextsketch.com` mailto path. **CURRENT.**
- **System of record** — the Google Sheet "Inbound Leads"; a capture succeeds only once its row is written (`07-technical-spec.md`). **CHANGED (new).**
- **Banned term** — language Brand Philosophy §8/§10 prohibits anywhere on the site. **CURRENT.**

## Roles and permissions — **CURRENT**

One human role: visitor (anonymous, public). One operator: owner (Nathan) — receives the lead alert and works the Sheet/Asana records; no admin UI exists. No accounts, no backend beyond `/api/qualify` (decision-log #8).

## 1. Modal — entry doors & question set

**Email first — CHANGED (decision-log #65, 2026-10-21).** The modal opens on **step 1** (Rule 1.7): first name, last name, email and at least one of the two offerings, nothing else (the names added by the owner the same day, before merge). Submitting it **saves the lead** (§2.4). **Step 2** follows in the same modal: the qualifier (Rules 1.1–1.4), then the contact step (1.5), **every one optional**, with "Skip for now" on every screen (1.6). What step 2 collects attaches to the step 1 lead (§2.4). **Supersedes the two-door model** of Sprint 02 Unit 04 (build-note 12): the side door into the qualifier ("Rather walk us through it?") is retired, since step 2 is that path. Both steps carry the §2.8 anti-bot guards, and every CTA still degrades to the mailto escape hatch with no JS (Rule E3).

**Step 2 asks, in order (canonical answer values in `06-taxonomy.md` §3). CHANGED (#65): the questions and answers are unchanged, every one is optional, and an unanswered one is passed over by Next:**

- **1.1 — Q1 Project type:** "What do you need built or improved?" → AI agents for the work my business already does / A new product built from scratch / Finishing a product someone else started / Ongoing help with a live product / I'm still exploring. **CHANGED (decision-log #53, 2026-10-15):** owner labels, agentic first; the question and the stored values are unchanged.
- **1.2 — Q2 Readiness:** "When are you looking to start?" → Ready now / Within 1–3 months / Just exploring
- **1.3 — Q3 Authority:** "Can you make the decision to move on this?" → Yes, it's my call / Shared decision / No — I'd need buy-in
- **1.4 — Q4 Validation:** "Are you open to validating the idea before we build?" → Already validated / Yes — that's why I'm here / I just want it built
- **1.5 — Contact step (step 2's last screen):** company and "tell us about it" free text (1000 char max), **both optional**. **CHANGED (#65, amended 2026-10-21):** the name and email are step 1's (first and last name, both required there) and are not asked again. A screen with nothing on it sends nothing (it ends on success).
- **1.6 — CHANGED (#65).** Every screen shows the escape hatch (Rule 2.6). Every step shows a back control **except step 2's first screen**, which follows a step already sent (there is nothing to go back to). **Every step 2 screen shows "Skip for now"** in the header beside the close control: it sends whatever step 2 has collected so far (nothing, if nothing) and ends on the success screen, or on the off-ramp if a collected answer is "still exploring" (Rule 2.1). Closing the modal mid step 2 keeps the lead and drops only the unsent answers. Any question may be passed over.
- **1.7 — Step 1, the quick door. CHANGED (decision-log #65, amended 2026-10-21).** Collects **first name and last name (both required, side by side at every width)**, **email (required)** and **"What do you need?" as two boxes, at least one required** (both is fine): "Agentic system integration" (`agentic`) and "AI product development" (`new_product`), the two offerings (#53, `06-taxonomy.md` §3). Nothing else is asked. Sending with no box checked is held back with "Pick at least one." under the boxes (builder DRAFT) and focus on the first box; a missing name or a missing or malformed email is held back by the browser's own validation. The Sheet keeps one Name column: the two names are written joined ("Jane Doe"). Every CTA that preselects a need checks one of these two boxes. Submit CTA "Talk to Us" (Rule 3.1 set). *(Before #65 it asked one required name, its boxes were optional, and the qualifier sat behind it as a second door; earlier still, the boxes replaced an optional one-line message. #65 as first written asked the email alone; the owner added the two names before merge.)*

## 2. Modal — branching and lead handling

- **2.1 — Off-ramp trigger:** IF Q1 = "I'm still exploring" OR Q2 = "Just exploring" THEN step 2 ends there: the answer, with those before it, is sent to the lead at once and the off-ramp screen is the ending; the remaining questions and the contact step are not shown. **CHANGED (#65):** the answer is **recorded on the lead** (`exploring` is now a stored value of Q1 and Q2, `06-taxonomy.md` §3) rather than refused by the schema. The lead already exists from step 1, so there is nothing for a direct POST to bypass. Answers past the trigger left from an earlier pass (E1) are not sent.
- **2.2 — Off-ramp content — CHANGED (#65).** The off-ramp stays affirming and non-apologetic ("You're not ready for this yet — and that's the right read…"), keeps the escape hatch visible, and is a **success state for the business, not an error state**: it is step 2's ending, with a Close control, like the success screen. Its optional "Stay in Touch" email capture and that capture's own confirmation screen are **retired**: step 1 already has the email. It remains **not a hard sell**.
- **2.3 — Qualified path:** all non-exploring qualifier combinations proceed through Q3, Q4, contact step. Q3 = "No — I'd need buy-in" and Q4 = "I just want it built" do NOT block submission — they are signals for Nathan, flagged in the alert (Rule 2.5). The site filters on exploration, not on imperfect answers. **CURRENT.**
- **2.4 — Submission & durable capture — CHANGED (#65).** **Step 1** is re-validated server-side and **durably recorded as one row in the Google Sheet "Inbound Leads" (the system of record), under a random lead ID the server mints, with a best-effort task in the Asana "Inbound Leads" project.** It reports success **only once the Sheet row is written** — **no fake success** (Rule 2.7) — and returns the lead ID to the browser. Resend then sends the lead **auto-reply** and Nathan's **alert**, best-effort and post-response. **Step 2** sends the lead ID and email back with whatever it collected; the Sheet's Apps Script finds the row whose lead ID **and** email match and **fills in that same row** (one visitor, one lead; the row's timestamp, email and lead ID never change). A step 2 send reports success only once the script confirms the update. If it added anything, Nathan gets **one short follow-up alert** pointing at the same lead; the visitor gets **no second auto-reply**, and step 2 creates no Asana task. There is **no database** (decision-log #8); the Sheet (+ Asana) is the record. *(Supersedes pre-pivot 2.4 "one email to hello@…, no data stored.")* **The waitlist band (CHANGED/new, decision-log #58)** captures through this same path, re-validation and Sheet gating included, as `kind: "waitlist"` (an email and the §2.8 guards, nothing else). It gets **no auto-reply**: its on-page confirmation ("You're on the list…") is the whole acknowledgement. **It gets no Asana task either** (owner direction, 2026-10-15): it is interest in a product, not work to pick up. Nathan's alert still fires, under the §2.5 label.
- **2.5 — Signal flags — CHANGED.** The lead record + alert subject carry a signal label:
  - **Step 1 only:** `[Lead — quick]`. **CHANGED (#65):** it now always means the visitor gave step 1 and nothing more.
  - **After step 2**, the row's label is recomputed from the answers, first match wins: `[Lead — exploring]` if Q1 or Q2 is "still exploring" · `[Lead — review answers]` if Q3 = `none` and Q4 = `build_first` · `[Lead — needs buy-in]` if Q3 = `none` · `[Lead — build-first mindset]` if Q4 = `build_first` · `[Lead]` when all four questions are answered · **`[Lead — partial]`** when any was passed over (new, #65). So any label but `[Lead — quick]` means step 2 added something. The follow-up alert's subject carries the recomputed label, the email and "(follow-up)".
  - The exploring auto-reply variant is **retired** (#65): the one auto-reply goes out on step 1, before any answer exists.
  - Waitlist band: **`[Waitlist]`** (`lead_type: waitlist`, decision-log #58). Deliberately not a `[Lead …]` label, so a waitlist entry reads apart from a lead in the Sheet and the alert subject. **CHANGED (new).**
- **2.6 — Escape hatch:** `hello@nextsketch.com` is visible in the modal at every step, on the off-ramp, and on the success screen — and in a site footer if one ever ships (no footer is in the redesign section set, decision-log #13). It is a plain mailto link — never a form. **CURRENT.**
- **2.7 — Failure fallback:** if a capture does not durably succeed (validation reject, destination unreachable/unconfigured, or network error), show the escape hatch with the user's composed answers preserved on screen so nothing is lost. Never silently drop a lead, and never show success for a lead that wasn't stored. **CURRENT** (now backed by the real no-fake-success gating in `/api/qualify`). **Since #65** the same fallback covers a failed step 2 send, with every answer (step 1's email and offerings included) preserved and in the mailto; the lead itself is already saved, and Back returns to the screen that sent. The waitlist band shows **its own** fallback (owner copy, 2026-10-15: "That didn't go through." / "Something failed on our end, not yours. Email us and we'll add you to the list.") on the modal's mechanics: its email preserved on screen and the escape hatch carrying it, under the subject "Add me to the NextSketch waitlist" (decision-log #58).
- **2.8 — Spam — CHANGED.** Both modal steps (#65) and the waitlist band carry a **honeypot** field (`_hp`, must be empty) + a **minimum-time check** (`_t` ≥ 3000ms; reject sub-3s completions, timed from the modal opening or the band mounting), enforced server-side. No CAPTCHA (friction budget is spent on qualification, not bot tests). **Rate limiting (the pre-pivot "5/hour per IP") was never implemented** — in-memory per-IP limiting is unreliable on Vercel serverless; deferred to its own unit with a shared store if abuse appears (build-note 09 §3). It is not a leak-stopper.

## 3. Language rules (site-wide, enforced at build and in verification) — **CURRENT**

- **3.1 — Required CTA set (exhaustive):** "Start a Conversation" · "Talk to Us" · "Qualify Your Project" · "Let's See if We're a Fit" · "Build With Us". Primary CTA: "Start a Conversation" (the hero CTA). The quick-door submit uses "Talk to Us" (in the set). **Carve-outs (CHANGED):** the off-ramp's **"Stay in Touch"** capture CTA was the first, and **retired with the capture** (#65). Step 2's **"Skip for now"** is not a conversion CTA (it ends the optional questions) and sits outside the set by nature. The waitlist band's **"Join the Waitlist"** is the other carve-out (decision-log #58): it asks for interest in a product that does not exist yet, so a conversion CTA would promise the wrong thing.
- **3.2 — Banned terms (grep targets):** Get Started · Free Consultation · Book a Call · Schedule a Call · Request a Quote · Contact Us · Learn More · AI-powered · full-service agency · end-to-end solutions · award-winning · passionate about · let's explore · let's chat · innovative solutions · digital transformation · leverage AI. **CHANGED (decision-log #54, 2026-10-15):** "automation" and "automations" are lifted from the list and the gate: it is the word buyers search for. Every other term still fails the build.
- **3.3 — Vocabulary — CHANGED (decision-log #54; the levels, #69):** **"agentic system" names the offer; "AI automation" describes it.** "Embedded agents" stays available; "we stay" / "partner, not a vendor". **"From idea to production" is retired from the vocabulary** (its last live uses were replaced by #59). **The five levels of AI are vocabulary now (#69), in the doc's words only:** Level 1 **Chatting with AI**, Level 2 **AI automations**, Level 3 **Integrating AI into your systems**, Level 4 **Internal tools**, Level 5 **Custom agentic systems** (`docs/five-levels-messaging.md`). Always "Level 2", never a renamed or shortened level. Use the doc's words, never its "instead of" column: not "Claude on a schedule", "Claude in your systems", "always-on agents" as a level of its own (always-on agents are a feature of internal tools), "custom systems", or a vendor's name in a level's name. Agentic System Integration covers Levels 2 and 3, AI Product Development covers Levels 4 and 5, and Level 1 is not sold. **"Internal tools" names Level 4 under AI Product Development (#72); "Internal Tool" as an offer of its own retired with #53 and does not return, and the waitlist band's installable product (#58) is neither.** Email triage is a **morning sort** (Level 2, #71), never an agent that reads every email as it arrives. The doc's other avoid-list items bind too: no hype words (guarantee, guaranteed, 10x, revolutionary, magic), no en dashes in copy as well as no em dashes (#19), "NextSketch" as one word, and exact site prices, never "about 3k a month".
- **3.4 — Retired brand sweep — CHANGED (decision-log #20, 2026-08-24; the exemption RETIRED by #63 as amended, 2026-10-15):** "Autonomous Whales", "industrial design", "mechanical design", "manufacturability" must not appear **as a current service claim**. That is what the rule was written to stop: the retired consultancy's service lines reappearing as things NextSketch sells today.
  - **Biography can be exempt, by ratified phrase only.** Copy that says where someone *trained*, not what NextSketch offers, is the opposite use. Exemptions are not granted to a term in general — they are granted to one exact phrase, in one named file, at an asserted occurrence count.
  - **Enforced in `scripts/check-banned-terms.mjs`** via its `ALLOWED` list, which is the source of truth for what is currently sanctioned. **Today the list is empty.** Its one entry, the About biography's `"I started in industrial design"` (scoped to `src/content/copy.ts`, count 1, #20), retired on 2026-10-15 when the owner took Nathan's biography out of the About section (#63, amended): the entry would have failed the gate as stale, which is the mechanism working as designed. With no entry, every term above fails in every file, About included. While an entry exists, the same clause in any other content file still fails the build, and so does rewording the sanctioned paragraph, so an exemption can never outlive the copy it was granted for.
  - Adding an entry is an owner decision and gets a decision-log row. The gate is the enforcement, this rule is the intent, and they are meant to agree — if they ever disagree, the gate wins and this rule is wrong.

## Edge cases — **CURRENT**

- **E1** Visitor goes back from the contact step and changes Q1 to "still exploring" → re-evaluate Rule 2.1, record Q1 (and nothing after it) on the lead, end on the off-ramp. **CHANGED (#65):** the answer is now recorded.
- **E2** Repeat visitor who hit the off-ramp → no cooldown; the modal is always available fresh.
- **E3** JavaScript disabled → the hero CTA degrades to the mailto escape hatch.
