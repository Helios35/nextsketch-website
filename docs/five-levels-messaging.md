## The story

Most businesses stop at Level 1: chatting with AI. NextSketch takes them to Levels 2 and 3 with automations, then to a tool they own, then to a full custom system.

The website is the baseline. Every post, ad and pitch about the levels uses the site's offer names and prices.

Approved headlines (from the Instagram post options):

- The 5 levels of AI in business.
- Where does your business sit?
- From one ask to a whole system.

Approved sublines:

- Most businesses stop at Level 1. We help you climb.
- Most businesses stop at Level 1. We take you further.

## The 5 levels

Level 1 is where most people already are. NextSketch's work starts at Level 2.

| Level | Name | One line | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chatting with AI | You ask, it answers. You do the rest. | Drafts, questions, summaries in ChatGPT or Claude |
| 2 | AI automations | Runs on a schedule. Nobody has to start it. | Morning inbox sort, weekly report, follow-ups |
| 3 | Integrating AI into your systems | Reads and updates the tools you already use. | Quotes and intake, data entry, CRM updates |
| 4 | Internal tools | Software you own, with always-on agents. | Your own dashboard, 24/7 agents, phone and chat |
| 5 | Custom agentic systems | Software your team and clients use. | Client portals, full apps, agent teams |

How to tell them apart:

- Level 1 vs 2: who presses go. At Level 1 a person asks. At Level 2 nobody asks: it runs on a timer and a person approves.
- Level 2 vs 3: Level 3 changes things inside the business's own tools (CRM, calendar, files, accounting), not just writes text.
- Level 3 vs 4: Level 4 is software the business owns, running in the cloud even when nobody is at a desk. Always-on agents are a feature of internal tools, not a level of their own.
- Level 4 vs 5: Level 5 is used by the whole team and by the business's own clients.

## Levels to offers

Levels 2 and 3 are the automations offer. Levels 4 and 5 are the product offer. Level 1 is not sold.

| Levels | Offer (site name) | Upfront | Monthly | What the site says it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Not sold | None | None | Where most businesses stop |
| 2 and 3 | Agentic System Integration | $3,998 | $2,998 | AI automation for the work you already do |
| 4 and 5 | AI Product Development | $12,000 | $10,000 | New software with AI agents built in |

The monthly fee is required for the first three months on both offers, then optional. Site examples for Agentic System Integration: email triage, weekly reporting, quoting and intake, document and data entry. Site paths into AI Product Development: build from scratch (live in 6 to 8 weeks), finish what someone started, ongoing product team. Source: NextSketch pricing.

## Words to use and avoid

| Use | Instead of | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AI automations | Claude on a schedule | Nate's call: the old name was unclear |
| Integrating AI into your systems | Claude in your systems | Nate's wording, closer to the site |
| Internal tools | Always-on agents (as a level) | Always-on agents are a feature of internal tools |
| Custom agentic systems | Custom systems | Matches the site's agentic language |
| AI (general) | Claude in level names | Not tied to one vendor; site says "Claude or OpenAI agents" |
| NextSketch (one word) | Next Sketch | Matches the site and logo |
| $2,998 a month | "about 3k a month" | Use the exact site price |

Also avoid:

- Hype words: guarantee, guaranteed, 10x, revolutionary, magic.
- Em dashes and en dashes in any copy.
- "Internal Tool" as a separate $7,998 offer: it came off the site; internal tools now sit under AI Product Development.
- Prices in paid Meta ads: the ad rule still stands. Organic posts may show site prices.

## Visual style

Each level keeps one color and one shape everywhere, taken from the Sketch OS design system.

| Level | Color | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blue #5579c6 | Circle |
| 2 | Amber #ab6b0c | Square |
| 3 | Reddish-brown #b95d45 | Triangle |
| 4 | Purple #9a62ab | Diamond |
| 5 | Olive green #66892f | Hexagon |

- Accents: coral #f26a4b for loops, arrows and highlights. Green #4f7a3a (#7fa85e on dark) marks Agentic System Integration. Dark yellow #9a6b10 (#c99a2e on dark) marks AI Product Development.
- Backgrounds: linen #e9e4d8 for light posts, #141414 for dark posts (matches the site).
- Fonts: Playfair Display for headlines, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers, Caveat for handwritten notes.
- Logo: the pencil NextSketch wordmark from the site. Ink version on light, cream version on dark.
- Sketch look (from the site's hero video): coral dashed hand-drawn loops, handwritten notes with drawn arrows, a dot grid.
- People: Asha and Nate, blurred in the background, generated in Higgsfield. Text and graphics sit in front.
- Level 1 as the outlier: a hollow dashed step, dimmer text, a dashed line between Level 1 and Level 2, and "you are here" next to "we start here".
