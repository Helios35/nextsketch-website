## Design feeling (binding, from Brand Philosophy §9) — **CURRENT**

Builders not talkers · confident restraint · feels like a product, not a brochure · grounded confidence. Nothing decorative for its own sake — every animation must communicate something. The dark cinematic language expresses this north star: a fast, product-like screen, not a marketing scroll.

## How to build a new section from this doc

1. Surface: the page is `background`; a section sits directly on it, and an elevated block is a solid **`card` with a `border` hairline** (§Surfaces). Corners follow fin's radius scale from one `--radius` token: controls `rounded-md`, cards and panels `rounded-xl`, sub-panels `rounded-lg` (#48). A bare `rounded` compiles to nothing: always name a step.
2. Type: one **display heading** (Playfair Display, `font-serif font-bold tracking-tight`), **mono micro-labels** (JetBrains Mono, uppercase, tracked, `muted-foreground`) for structure and meta, **body and card titles** in Inter, and every figure in Inter `tabular-nums` (§Typography).
3. Color: `foreground` linen on `background`, `muted-foreground` for secondary text; **coral `brand` is the only accent** and is spent sparingly: payoff words, selection, markers, links (§Color). A coral fill always carries `brand-foreground`.
4. Layout: full-width band, shared gutters, generous bottom-weighted vertical padding (§Layout).
5. Motion: CSS keyframes only, `motion-safe`-gated, **200ms or less** for anything that is not ambient, and reduced motion removes all of it (§Motion).
6. Controls: the stone divided-arrow `primary` button advances, the `secondary` button steps back, selection fills flat coral, and every focus ring is `ring` (§Interaction vocabulary).
7. Diagrams: any visual that explains how something works is drawn in the diagram language, from its shared primitives (§Diagrams, #60). There is no other illustration style.

If a rule here doesn't cover your case, generalize from the hero/modal code and flag the judgment call — don't invent a new pattern silently.

## Color & theme

Token **names** are fin's (decision-log **#47**) and canonical per `06-taxonomy.md` §5. They are defined once in `src/app/globals.css` (the source of truth) the way fin builds them: raw custom properties on `:root`, then an `@theme inline` bridge that republishes each into Tailwind's `--color-*` namespace, so every utility in a component resolves to one of them. Tailwind's stock colour, radius, shadow, text-shadow and drop-shadow namespaces are cleared (`--color-*: initial` and its siblings), so nothing off this list compiles. **Every colour on the site is declared in `globals.css` and nowhere else**: a component never writes a hex, an `rgb()` or a named colour. The only literals outside `:root` are transparencies of black (the shadow scale, the over-video shadows, the hero mask) and the dialog backdrop (below).

**Dark only (#47).** Fin's `.dark` set is the site's only set and lives on `:root`; fin's light set is not used, there is no theme toggle, and `html` declares `color-scheme: dark` so native scrollbars and form controls render dark. Scrollbars are thin, `border` on a transparent track (fin, base layer). A bare `border` draws in the `border` token, never `currentColor` (fin's base-layer rule).

### The text ladder and surface steps: **CHANGED (decision-log #47; replaces the alpha ladders)**

The white-on-black alpha ladders are **RETIRED** (recorded under §Retired). The system now has two validated text steps and a run of solid surface steps:

- **Text: two steps, no others.** `foreground` for everything that was `white` through `white/70` (headings, body, values, option labels, strip labels); `muted-foreground` for everything secondary (captions, eyebrows, labels, placeholders, metadata, muted controls, decorative numerals). There is no dimmer legal text step, so a numeral that sat at `white/20` now meets 4.5:1 at `muted-foreground`. Do not dim text with alpha or opacity; the one sanctioned dimming is a disabled control at `opacity-50` (§Interaction vocabulary).
- **Surface steps, darkest to lightest:** `sidebar` `#101010` (unused) · `background` `#141414` (the page) · `card` `#1C1C1C` (elevated) · `secondary` `#222222` (inside a card) · `muted` `#2A2A2A` (decorative fills) · `accent` `#363636` (hover fill). Adjacent steps sit a few RGB points apart, too close to separate on their own: **the `border` hairline is what separates a surface from what it sits on** (fin), and every lifted surface carries one.
- **Hairlines: one colour, `border`.** Hover lifts a card's or a row's border to `muted-foreground/50`; a row hover never changes its fill.
- **Alpha on `background` (image treatment and backdrops):** `background/25` flat over the hero footage plus `bg-hero-scrim` (§Hero loop) · `background/85 → /20 → transparent` scrim on each About avatar (the portrait's treatment, carried over by #63) · `background/45` grade over a Work screenshot (`/50` on a near-white one), clearing to `/5` on hover, with a `from-card/90` scrim (the card's fill, not `background`) on its lower two thirds · `background/40` + `backdrop-blur-sm` on the capability strip. The modal backdrop is `background` at 72% plus an 8px blur, written as the literal `rgb(20 20 20 / 0.72)` in `globals.css` because `::backdrop` did not inherit custom properties in the verification browser.

## Surfaces

Three surface levels, every one separated from what it sits on by a **`border` hairline**, not by shadow (fin: in dark, a shadow barely registers):

1. **The page**: flat `background`, on every route, the 404 included. Sections sit directly on it, with **no divider hairlines between sections** (owner call, 2026-07-06, stands).
2. **Image band**: the home hero only: the footage under a flat `background/25` overlay **plus** the `bg-hero-scrim` gradient, the whole wrapper under the `hero-fade` mask, so the band dissolves into the page on all four edges with no hard stop (§Hero loop; #50). Images inside a card (a Work screenshot, an About avatar) take the alphas listed in §Color.
3. **Card**: the reusable elevated surface:
   - **Fill:** solid **`bg-card`**, the default everywhere: the home Services cards (their leftover glass was dropped by #47, closing that #46 open item), the Work cards, the pricing tiers, the service rows, the How-it-works cards and the diagrams' panels. **Glass** (`bg-card/95 backdrop-blur-xl`) only where something passes behind the surface: the nav bar past 80px, the nav overlay, and the modal card from `md:`. The capability strip is the lighter in-flow glass, `bg-background/40 backdrop-blur-sm`.
   - **Border:** `border border-border` (card) or `border-y border-border` (full-width strip). Hover lifts a card to `hover:border-muted-foreground/50` at 150ms: a colour change, never a fill or a transform.
   - **Depth:** fin's soft shadow scale, `shadow-xs` to `shadow-2xl` (positive spread, a blur about three to five times the offset, 4 to 9% black; only `shadow-2xl` has a live use). In dark it barely shows, which is why a lifted surface always carries a border too. Live uses, all `shadow-2xl`: the modal card (from `md:`), the diagrams' agent hub chip and the documents in the document-entry pile (§Diagrams, #60). The Product Support mock's selected task card retired with the wireframes, and the app cluster's hub tile with the cluster (2026-10-15). No glows: depth comes from border and shadow, never from coloured light. (**Narrowed by #35, 2026-08-31:** this rule governs *component* depth and still binds there; `/pricing` and the service routes carry an owner-directed page-background glow, `page-glow.tsx` with `bg-page-glow`, coral since #47, which is a page backdrop, not component light.)
   - **Sub-panels** inside a card (the modal's summary list): `rounded-lg border border-border bg-secondary`, padded `p-5`; option rows and inputs are `secondary` at `rounded-md` (§Interaction vocabulary). ## Spacing, layout & responsive rhythm

Generalized from the hero's composition and the modal's density:

- **Gutter rhythm (binding):** horizontal padding steps `px-6 → sm:px-8 → lg:px-16`. Every full-width band uses these gutters; panel interiors use `px-6 → md:px-10`.
- **Full-bleed, bottom-weighted:** the hero is `min-h-dvh`, `flex-col items-start justify-end` — content anchors to the **bottom-left**, with heavy bottom padding stepping `pb-16 → sm:pb-24 → lg:pb-28`. - **Two-column row:** content splits `flex-col gap-6 → sm:flex-row sm:items-end`, each column `w-full sm:w-1/2` — headline/action left, supporting matter right (right-aligned from `sm:` up). Collapse to a single column below `sm`.
- **Measure:** a secondary strip/band caps at `max-w-4xl`; an elevated card caps at `md:max-w-[560px]` and centers.
- **Vertical cadence inside a block:** `space-y-6` between heading / body / action at hero scale; the modal's denser form cadence is `gap-5` fields, `mt-6` body-to-form, `mt-8` to the action row.

## Motion

**The contract: CHANGED (binding; fin §8, adopted by decision-log #47).** All motion is **CSS**: keyframes declared in `globals.css` `@theme`, applied **`motion-safe:` only**. **Every transition and entrance runs in 200ms or less, or not at all.** **Reduced motion removes all of it, stated once:** a base-layer block under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every `transition-duration` and `transition-delay` to `0s` and every animation to `0.01ms` with no delay and a single iteration (so any `animationend` still fires), and turns `scroll-behavior` to `auto`, all `!important`, so no component has to remember.
